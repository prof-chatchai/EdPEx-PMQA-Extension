Manual for assessing the status of government agencies as Bureaucracy 4.0,
under the government performance-assessment framework, fiscal year B.E. 2568 (2025).

[...] within the agency and the agency's efforts to drive its work toward sustainable goals.
The OPDC (Office of the Public Sector Development Commission) assesses Bureaucracy 4.0 status as the overall
average score across all 7 categories: Category 1 Organizational Leadership, Category 2 Strategic Planning,
Category 3 Customer and Stakeholder Focus, Category 4 Measurement, Analysis and Knowledge Management,
Category 5 Workforce Focus, Category 6 Operations Focus, and Category 7 Results.
The assessment criteria are those of round 2 (12 months), 2025 (criteria raised to approximate the PMQA 4.0
award criteria of 2024), and targets are set in line with the PMQA 4.0 development levels shown below.

KPI details
KPI: assessment of the agency's status as a Bureaucracy 4.0 organization (PMQA 4.0)
National strategy: rebalancing and developing the public-sector management system
Master plan: issue 20, public services and public-sector efficiency
Sub-master plan: development of the public-sector management system
Sub-master-plan target: the public sector has high capability, equivalent to international standards
Target for 2022-2024 (using the PMQA 4.0 criteria of 2023): 90 percent -> 100 percent [table, partly OCR noise]

Target for 2025: assessment criteria round 2 (12 months), 2025 (raised to approximate the PMQA 4.0 award criteria of 2024)
Departments, provinces and public organizations:
  Initial target (50) = 300 points; standard target (75) = 400 points; advanced target (100) = 470 points.

The OPDC designed the tool for assessing an organization's status as Bureaucracy 4.0 (PMQA 4.0) to serve the
agency's mission and to link it to national strategy and to results in national competitiveness, by
understanding the challenges of both the agency and the country's development direction so as to set
challenging strategic goals. There are 3 development levels:
(1) Basic: assessment result equivalent to 300-399 points;
(2) Advance: assessment result equivalent to 400-469 points;
(3) Significance: assessment result equivalent to 470-500 points.
[OCR noise]
Basic level: systematic but reactive operation, i.e. performing duties according to function and regulations.
Advance level: proactive operation, i.e. thinking and analysing before acting and anticipating events that may occur.
Significance level (continuous development): effective and innovative operation, i.e. thinking in a connected,
integrated way to produce effective and innovative results. The sub-dimensions are described below.


===== page 8 =====
Manual for assessing the status of government agencies as Bureaucracy 4.0,
under the government performance-assessment framework, fiscal year B.E. 2568 (2025).

The OPDC set the standard for the PMQA 4.0 (KPI) assessment in 2025 under the Bureaucracy 4.0 status-assessment
criteria, revised edition B.E. 2567 (2024), together with the training material on assessing government
agencies' Bureaucracy 4.0 status held on 15-16 January 2025. The score level of each item is judged from the
results the agency writes in answer to that item. The main overall assessment guidelines are:
[OCR noise]
- Adhere to the principle of Learning and improvement relative to the previous year.
- The written answer to each item should explain "A-D-R", i.e. the system/approach (Approach), its deployment
  (Deploy) illustrated with concrete examples, and the results (Result); early results may be reported when the
  item has no measured results yet.
- The Organizational Profile (OP) submitted by the agency is related to each question in the process
  categories (Categories 1 to 6) and the results category (Category 7), for example:

Links between the Organizational Profile (OP) and the process categories (Categories 1 to 6) and the results
category (Category 7)
[figure labels:] PROCESS; technology-innovation-network development plan; related criteria/categories;
culture/law; challenges, advantages; budget/revenue; customers, stakeholders; outputs, services; service
technology; workforce, workforce changes; internal risks; suppliers, partners, collaborators; outputs,
laws and regulations, core competencies; performance improvement. [partly OCR noise]

The assessment considers the Organizational Profile information submitted by the agency when scoring the answers
to related questions, as shown in the figure linking the OP with the process categories (Categories 1 to 6) and
the results category (Category 7). Scoring of some questions therefore differs between agencies because they
cannot all be viewed the same way; e.g. sub-item 1.1.1, on the social impact of the agency's core mission, is
judged on whether the impact on society is linked to the nature of the agency's core-mission work.

- The score level of each item is judged from whether the item's review points for organizational development
  are fully met, placing it at one of the 3 PMQA 4.0 development levels; the total score is the sum of the
  averages at each level, namely:
  1) Basic = 300 points: systematic but reactive operation (printed "Proactive" in the source), i.e. performing
     duties according to function and regulations.
  2) Advance = 400 points (300 plus another 100 points): proactive operation, i.e. thinking and analysing before
     acting and anticipating events that may occur.
  3) Significance = 500 points (400 plus another 100 points): effective and innovative operation
     (Effective & Innovation), i.e. thinking in a connected, integrated way to produce effective and innovative results.
